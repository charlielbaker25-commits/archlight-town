# Art credits

The 3D village in Reaper HQ uses only these free kits by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), all released under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**: free to use, including commercially, with no attribution required. We credit
Kenney anyway. Each kit's own `License.txt` is kept in its folder.

| Folder | Kit | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `city-kit-commercial/` | City Kit (Commercial) | the agents' buildings, awnings and parasols |
| `city-kit-suburban/` | City Kit (Suburban) | houses, trees, planters and fences around the districts |
| `city-kit-roads/` | City Kit (Roads) | roads, street lamps, cones, barriers and signs |
| `hexagon-kit/` | Hexagon Kit | the hex tiles of the districts, the streets and the grass |
| `mini-characters/` | Mini Characters | the townspeople who walk round working buildings and carry messages |

Only the GLB files (and each kit's one colour-map texture) are kept. Nothing is from the Black Ops games or any
other copyrighted source. The orb, the message arcs, glows and rings are drawn by the page's own code.
